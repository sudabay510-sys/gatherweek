# Gatherweek — Brand identity

**Slug:** `gatherweek` · **Tagline:** A weekly volunteer and announcements plan for small communities.
**Status:** Early access. Checkout not live; founding list only. No payments processed.

## Positioning
Volunteer coordinators and admins at faith communities, mutual-aid groups, PTAs, and small nonprofits.

**Promise (honest):** Coordination help only. No attendance, donation, or fundraising promises.

## Personas
- **Volunteer coordinator:** You fill the schedule — often by texting the same five people.
- **Part-time admin:** The bulletin, the email, the slides — all on you.
- **New group organizer:** Starting a group and want good habits from day one.

## Customer journey the landing page follows
1. **Collect** — pain: Updates arrive at the last minute. → plan: One weekly collection day and form.
2. **Announce** — pain: Different message on every channel. → plan: One template, sent everywhere.
3. **Staff** — pain: No-shows and the same few helpers. → plan: Micro-roles, reminders, and swaps.
4. **Welcome** — pain: Newcomers drift away. → plan: A buddy and a 3-message welcome sequence.

## Voice & tone
Warm, respectful, inclusive. Speaks to every tradition without assuming one.
- Short, concrete sentences. Checklists over hype.
- Never shame, scare, or invent urgency.

## Logo
`static/logo.svg` — monogram mark on a rounded tile in the primary accent. Minimum size 24px; clear space ≈ 25% of the mark height. Use on light backgrounds; on dark, keep the tile as-is.

## Pricing (planned terms; not live)
- **4-week rhythm** — $12 first 4 weeks, then $15 / 4 weeks unless cancelled
- **12-week season** — $29 first 12 weeks, then $39 / 12 weeks unless cancelled
- **Annual (org)** — $99 first year, then $129 / year unless cancelled

## Do
- Use neutral language that fits any faith or secular group.
- Emphasize consent and privacy.
- Show intro **and** renewal prices before any future charge.
- Label the founding list as “no charge today”.
- Keep the disclaimer visible near every plan.

## Don’t
- Assume a specific religion or use political messaging.
- Promise attendance, donations, or fundraising results.
- Fake testimonials, user counts, stats, or press logos.
- Countdown timers, “spots left”, or other urgency/scare copy.
